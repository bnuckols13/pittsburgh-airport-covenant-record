Plate I

What the Authority itself has reported, and what it has not

The line joins only the Authority's own documents on its own residual basis. Newspaper figures and the federal Form 5100-127 filing are drawn as marks and are never joined to it, because a clip may be an utterance and never a figure, and because the two bases are two series. Years the Authority has not published on this basis are gaps, never drawn through: none, the table is continuous. The line is one table, the five-year statement at os-2025ab PDF 62. Where a second Authority document gives a different figure for a year already on it, that figure is a mark beside the line and not a point on it: 2021 $12.33 (Lowered to, as the Authority reported it); 2024 $11.95 (Budgeted). Plate XI

What Blue Sky printed about the charge, and what was filed

Blue Sky News is the Authority's own outlet. It printed a figure for the cost per enplaned passenger in two posts, Oct. 19, 2018 ($10.35) and Nov. 18, 2019 ($9.29), both budget projections, not reported results. The 26 later posts that describe what airlines pay at Pittsburgh, the last on Oct. 11, 2025, print no figure for the charge. The filed lines are the charge reported in the 2025 bond statement for 2020 to 2024 and the airport consultant's forecasts in the 2021, 2023 and 2025 statements, one table per line. Blue Sky's figures appear as text on the lower strip and are never placed on the dollar scale or joined to a line. The plate covers Blue Sky only and says nothing about what the Authority told other outlets.

Plate X

Boardings by airline, 2019 to 2024

Between 2019 and 2024 the four legacy carriers boarded 217 thousand fewer passengers at Pittsburgh and the low-cost carriers 313 thousand more, led by Spirit, up 194 thousand. The airport as a whole boarded 89 thousand more. Spirit shut down nationwide in May 2026 after two bankruptcies; the record does not connect its shutdown to the charge at this airport, and this plate does not say it does. Orange marks a decline, not a loss to anyone.
