Plate I

What the Authority itself has reported, and what it has not

The line joins only the Authority's own documents on its own residual basis. Newspaper figures and the federal Form 5100-127 filing are drawn as marks and are never joined to it, because a clip may be an utterance and never a figure, and because the two bases are two series. Years the Authority has not published on this basis are gaps, never drawn through: none, the table is continuous. The line is one table, the five-year statement at os-2025ab PDF 62. Where a second Authority document gives a different figure for a year already on it, that figure is a mark beside the line and not a point on it: 2021 $12.33 (Lowered to, as the Authority reported it); 2024 $11.95 (Budgeted). Vertical scale $8.69 to $21.65.
