The covenant, decomposed

The Official Statements print one combined ratio. This separates it into the two things it combines.

The covenant as written lets the Coverage Account count toward the 1.25 test, and on that test every forecast year complies. No payment is missed in any forecast year. The column that matters is the last but one: what the ratio is on pledged Net Revenues alone, before the discretionary deposit. 11 of 14 forecast years come in under 1.25 on that basis.
