Recorded 2026-08-31 from `os-2025ab`, hashed in the vault. The case rule holds: where a
document contradicts itself, disclose the conflict and do not pick a figure. Each entry
below was reproduced from the PDF during this pass, except the last, which was not.

| Page | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 |

any claim that $16.10 is the highest charge in the airport's history: on either page the
